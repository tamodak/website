Hisar data safety

Last updated 13 October 2026

Hisar's Data safety section on Google Play says:

  • No data collected
  • No data shared with third parties
  • Committed to follow the Play Families Policy

This page explains those answers and goes through each type of data, so you can check them for yourself. The privacy policy has the full details.

Why “no data collected”

Google counts data as collected when an app sends it off the phone, and as shared when it goes on to someone else. Hisar sends nothing to tamodak, and tamodak runs no server for it. What Hisar does send goes straight to services you use through it, and Google's rules don't count it as collection or sharing:

  • Browsing the web. Pages you open, searches and translated pages go to those websites, as in any browser. Google's rules exclude data sent while browsing the open web.
  • Things you start yourself. Search suggestions send what you type in the address bar to your search engine, and you can turn them off. Image search sends the one photo you choose to Google Lens. Google's rules exclude transfers you start and expect.

Type by type

Location
Not accessed. Hisar has no location permission, and websites that ask are refused.
Personal info
Name, email, address and phone number are never asked for. What you type into a website goes only to that website.
Financial info
Not accessed. Payments you make on a website go only to that website.
Health, messages, contacts and calendar
Not accessed.
Photos and videos
Only the photo you take or choose for image search is sent, to Google Lens. Pictures on web pages are judged by the picture filter on the phone and never uploaded.
Audio
Never recorded by Hisar. Voice search runs in your phone's own speech service, and Hisar receives only the text.
Files
Downloads and offline pages are saved on the phone.
Web browsing history
Stays on the phone and is left out of backups. You can delete it with Delete browsing data.
Searches and typed text
Searches go to your search engine (Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo). If suggestions are on, typed text goes to it after a pause in typing. Text that contains a blocked word is never sent, and a blocked search never leaves the phone.
Crash logs and diagnostics
Hisar sends none. Android itself may send crash reports to Google Play if you allowed that in your phone's settings.
Device and advertising IDs
Not used. Hisar reads no advertising ID, IMEI, serial number or network identifiers.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load web pages.
  • Storage, only on Android 9 and older: to save downloads.

Hisar asks for nothing else. For image search it opens your camera app or the photo picker instead of asking for camera or storage access.

Security

  • Hisar's own connections (suggestions, image search, model downloads) use HTTPS, and the picture filter's model files are checked against their SHA-256 checksums.
  • Your data stays in Hisar's private storage and is left out of Android backups and transfers to a new phone. Uninstalling Hisar deletes it.
  • Hisar is open source. Its code shows exactly what it sends.
