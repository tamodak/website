Hisar privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Hisar is a safe browser for Android made by tamodak. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

In short: Hisar has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. tamodak runs no server for Hisar, so nothing about you, your phone or what you browse ever reaches us. What you do in Hisar stays on your phone, apart from the connections any browser makes, which are listed below.

What stays on your phone

  • Your browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs and tab groups, saved offline pages and settings.
  • Cookies, site data and cached files that websites store.
  • All filtering. Addresses, page text, search results and pictures are checked on the phone. A blocked site is stopped before any request is made, so it never leaves the phone. Searches go out, and what comes back is filtered. The word and site lists are built into the app; the ad blocker's lists also update themselves (see below).
  • The picture filter. Its AI models run on the phone, and no picture you see is sent anywhere to be judged.

Hisar keeps this data in its private app storage. It's left out of Android backups and transfers to a new phone. Settings → Privacy and security → Delete browsing data clears history, cookies, site data and cache. Uninstalling Hisar deletes all of it, except files you downloaded to your Downloads folder.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

Websites you visit

Like every browser, Hisar connects to the websites you open. They receive what any website receives, such as your IP address, the browser's version and the cookies they set, and their own privacy policies apply. Hisar also asks Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Brave Search for their strict safe search.

Your search engine

Searches go to the engine you choose: Google (the default), Microsoft Bing or DuckDuckGo. By default, Hisar also sends what you type in the address bar to that engine to get suggestions, after a short pause in typing. Suggestions that contain a blocked word aren't shown. You can turn suggestions off with Settings → Autocomplete searches and URLs.

Image search (Google Lens)

Only when you take or choose a photo to search with: Hisar sends that photo, reduced to at most 1000 pixels, to Google Lens. Hisar sends it with the Google cookies stored in Hisar so the results open in the same session. If you're signed in to Google in Hisar, the search is linked to your Google account.

Voice search

Your phone's own speech recognition service handles voice search (usually Google's). Hisar never records audio. It receives only the recognised text.

Translate

When you choose to translate a page, Hisar opens its address in Google Translate.

Protection against dangerous sites

Hisar's browser engine, Mozilla GeckoView, downloads lists of known malware and phishing sites from Google Safe Browsing. If an address you open matches an entry, the engine asks Google for the full entries that share a short hash prefix. Google never receives the address itself. The engine may also download security data from Mozilla, such as lists of revoked certificates. These downloads are the same for every user and carry nothing about what you browse.

Picture filter models

On first start, Hisar downloads its picture filter's models once from the Hisar releases on GitHub. It uses Android's download manager and checks each file's SHA-256 checksum before using it. As with any download, GitHub sees your IP address.

Ad blocker filter lists

Hisar blocks ads with uBlock Origin, which is built into the app and decides on the phone what to block. It comes with its filter lists and keeps them up to date by downloading new versions from their publishers, some every 12 hours and others every few days: uBlock Origin's own lists and EasyList from GitHub, Cloudflare and jsDelivr; the Online Malicious URL Blocklist from GitHub, GitLab and Cloudflare; Peter Lowe's list from pgl.yoyo.org; the Public Suffix List from publicsuffix.org; and, if your phone's language has a list of its own, that list from its publisher (for Turkish, AdGuard). These downloads are the same for every user and carry nothing about you or what you browse. As with any download, those servers see your IP address.

Google Play

Google Play installs and updates Hisar under Google's own privacy policy. Through the Play Console, tamodak sees only the totals Google shows every developer: installs, ratings, reviews, and crash reports from people who chose to share diagnostics with app developers in their Android settings.

What Hisar doesn't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in to Hisar.
  • No ads, no advertising ID and no device identifiers (such as IMEI or serial numbers).
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reports sent by the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data. tamodak has none to sell or share.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions. Hisar asks only for internet access, plus storage on Android 9 and older to save downloads. To take a photo for image search, Hisar opens your camera app.
  • Websites get no access to your location, camera, microphone or notifications.

Children

Hisar is meant for everyone, including children, and follows Google Play's Families policy. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and uses no advertising ID or device identifiers. Websites and search engines a child uses have their own privacy policies. Hisar's filtering reduces what children can reach, but no filter is perfect, so parents and guardians should still stay involved.

Security

  • Your data stays in Hisar's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and left out of backups.
  • Hisar's own connections (search suggestions, image search, model downloads and filter list updates) use HTTPS.
  • Hisar is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. You can check every claim on this page in its source code.
  • To report a security problem, write to info@tamodak.org (see our security.txt).

Your rights

Data protection laws such as Turkey's KVKK and the EU's GDPR give you the right to access, correct and delete your personal data. tamodak holds no personal data from Hisar, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete. What Hisar keeps is on your phone and under your control. For data held by the services above, contact them directly: Google, Microsoft, DuckDuckGo, GitHub, GitLab, Cloudflare, jsDelivr, AdGuard and Mozilla.

Changes

If a Hisar version would send anything not listed here, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top. Every earlier version of this page is kept in the website's repository.
